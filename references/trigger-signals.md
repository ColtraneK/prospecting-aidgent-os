# Trigger signals — what makes someone worth opening this week

You judge fit; this is the taxonomy to judge WITH. A trigger is an observable
event that makes outreach timely. No trigger, no urgency — a perfect title who
has shown no signal belongs at the bottom of the list, not off it.

## The trigger taxonomy, strongest first

1. **Cross-post engagement (2+ touches) — highest intent.** The same person
   reacting or commenting on two or more posts about the ICP's pain, across
   creators or across weeks. One touch is curiosity; two is a live problem.
2. **Pain-point post.** They wrote, in their own words, about the exact
   problem the offer solves. The post IS the opener: quote it.
3. **Tool-recommendation ask.** "What do people use for X?" — they are
   actively shopping. Answer the question first; the value is the answer.
4. **Hiring signal.** Hiring for the function the offer replaces or augments
   ("looking for an ops manager") — budget exists and the pain is named.
5. **Funding / growth announcement.** New money means new build-out and new
   bottlenecks. Strong for timing, weak for personal connection — pair it
   with something they said, not just the announcement.
6. **Job change into the buying seat.** New COO/Head of Ops in their first
   ~90 days is rethinking everything. The window is short.
7. **Competitor-post engagement.** They engaged with a competitor's content:
   aware of the category, evaluating. Tread lightly — never mention the
   competitor.

## Recency tiers

- **Hot — under 24h.** Open these first; a comment today reads as presence,
  not surveillance.
- **Warm — under 7 days.** Still current; the post is quotable without
  explanation.
- **Cool — under 30 days.** Usable context, weak trigger. Do not open with a
  three-week-old post as if it were news.
- Older than 30 days is background, not a trigger.

## Reading an engager list (a post's reactors/commenters)

High-quality engager signals:
- an operator-level title in the ICP (the doer-buyer, not an intern);
- a company that fits the ICP's size and industry;
- posts themselves — an active poster can be quoted and re-engaged;
- their comment is thoughtful: adds an experience, disagrees usefully, asks a
  real question.

Low-quality engager signals (skip, whatever the title says):
- generic praise ("Great post!", "So true 🙌");
- template language that could sit under any post;
- the same copy-paste comment across many creators (engagement-pod tell);
- likers with no other visible activity anywhere.

## Nomination discipline

`why_nominated` states the trigger and its recency in one line:
"commented substantively on two capacity posts this week (cross-post, hot)".
If you cannot name the trigger, you are nominating a title, not a person —
expect the evidence to change your mind, and let it.
